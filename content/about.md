## About me

I'm a political and administrative science student with a focus on the Maghreb, currently finishing my studies at Spain's National University of Distance Education (UNED). I have previously studied at the Complutense University of Madrid and did a year abroad at the University of Geneva.

I was born in Madrid and have lived in Spain, Mauritania, and Switzerland. I am currently based in Germany. My roots are Sahrawi and Mauritanian, and that background shapes my interest in the politics, institutions and elections of the region.

I work with data, writing and open knowledge to make information on Maghreb politics more accessible, accurate and easy to verify. I have volunteered since the age of 17 with Africa Elects to help improve the coverage of Maghrebi politics—especially when it comes to election systems and results, together with political parties and their ideologies.

I also advocate for the right of my Sahrawi people to self-determination. I have participated at several conferences related to the matter, and spoken publicly on behalf of the Polisario Front—the internationally recognised Sahrawi national liberation movement.

## Beyond work
I am an editor at the English Wikipedia. My main focus is to improve Wikipedia's coverage of information about Western Sahara, Mauritania, and global election information.

I am very interested in language learning. I am a polyglot: I speak Hassaniya (native), Spanish (native-like), English (C2), Standard Arabic (C1), French (C1), Catalan (B2), and German (B1+). Language learning is one of the most effective ways for me to engage with and better understand other cultures and societies.

In my personal life, I enjoy music, reading, movies, travelling, and nature.
