## 4. Web analytics (GoatCounter)

I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to understand in aggregate how many people visit the site and which pages are read.

- GoatCounter does **not** use cookies and does **not** store your IP address or other personal identifiers in a way that allows you to be identified or tracked across sites or visits.
- Collected are only aggregated statistics, such as page visited, referrer, browser, device type, screen size and country.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the website).
- **More information:** [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/privacy)

You can prevent counting by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser or by using a content blocker.
